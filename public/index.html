The workflow

From “take a look” to
“we know what to build.”

01 / UPLOAD

Share the real thing

Upload your static HTML as a project. Every version and page gets its own URL, ready to revisit later.

02 / GUIDE

Show the intended flow

Record a walkthrough by using the prototype. Let it play when someone opens the page, then hand control back to them.

03 / DECIDE

Turn feedback into action

Pin comments, draw annotations, and accept the changes that should move forward. Developers get the agreed context.
